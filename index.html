zaizai:
Pet Agent

A tiny companion for Apple Watch that can sense the rhythm of your day — learning when to come closer, and when to stay quietly by your side.

Explore the concept ↓
a quiet presence
Product thesis

Not another pet to maintain. Not another agent waiting for commands.

zaizai explores a third kind of relationship: a companion with its own presence, shaped by real-life context and respectful initiative — without turning care into obligation.

01 / PET FIRST

A character, not a chatbot

Personality is felt through gaze, movement, timing and restraint — not long conversations.

02 / LOW BURDEN

Care without guilt

Ignoring an interaction is always valid. zaizai never turns absence into punishment.

03 / TIMING

Intelligence as discretion

The most important decision may be whether to appear at all.
